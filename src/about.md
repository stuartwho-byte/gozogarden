<h1>About this journal</h1>
<div class="page-body">

This is a working record of rehabilitating a walled garden at a property in St. Lawrenz, Gozo, Malta — limestone geology, alkaline soil, a clay hardpan sitting a few inches under most of the garden, and forty years of gardening instinct built for entirely different conditions (New York, North Carolina) that needed re-learning from scratch.

Posts go up a few times a year, roughly tracking the Mediterranean garden calendar — after the winter rains, going into summer dormancy, at the peak of summer stress, and coming out the other side in autumn. Each one covers what actually happened: what died, what the soil and water turned out to be doing, what got diagnosed correctly and what got diagnosed wrong first, and what's being tried next.

It's a journal before it's advice. If something here saves another Mediterranean-climate gardener a season of guessing, that's the bonus, not the point.

</div>
